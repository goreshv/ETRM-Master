Privacy Policy - ETRM Master

Last updated: March 22, 2026

1. Introduction

ETRM Master is an Energy Trading Risk Management application that allows users to simulate and manage energy trades. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide our services:

  • Trade Data: Company names, counterparties, products, prices, quantities, dates
  • App Usage Data: How you interact with the app features
  • Device Information: Device type and operating system for compatibility

3. How We Use Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide and maintain the ETRM Master app functionality
  • Store and manage your trade data securely
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Provide customer support

4. Data Storage and Security

Your trade data is stored securely on our cloud servers using industry-standard encryption and security measures. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties without your consent, except as described in this Privacy Policy.

6. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your trade data
  • Update or delete your information
  • Request data export
  • Opt-out of data collection

7. Children's Privacy

ETRM Master is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
