ETRM Master - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

ETRM Master ("the App") is developed and maintained by x24tech ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the ETRM Master mobile application.

1. Overview

ETRM Master is an educational Energy Trading & Risk Management simulation and learning platform. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy. The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or sensitive user data.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or government identification.
  • Location Data: The App does not access, collect, or monitor device location data (neither GPS nor network-based).
  • Financial & Payment Data: The App is free and does not process financial transactions or payment card data.
  • Biometric or Health Data: The App does not collect or access biometric or health data.
  • Device Identifiers: The App does not collect persistent device identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, advertising ID) for tracking purposes.

3. On-Device Local Data Storage

The App stores non-personal educational progress data locally on your device using Android's sandboxed local storage (AsyncStorage):

  • Quiz Progression: Category completion status and high scores (used to unlock subsequent quiz categories).
  • Curriculum State: Study module bookmarks and progress.

All local data remains exclusively on your device. It is never transmitted to external servers, cloud databases, or third parties. You can clear this data at any time in device app settings or via the "Reset Progress" button in the app.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

  • No Advertisements: The App contains no third-party ads or ad networks.
  • No Tracking SDKs: The App does not integrate third-party tracking, analytics, or profiling SDKs.

5. Permissions

The App requests no sensitive Android permissions (such as Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Location, SMS, or external storage). It functions completely offline.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not target or knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
