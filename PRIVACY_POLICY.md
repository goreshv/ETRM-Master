# Privacy Policy for ETRM Master

**Last updated:** September 29, 2026

**ETRM Master** ("the App") is developed and maintained by **x24tech** ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the ETRM Master mobile application.

---

### 1. Overview
ETRM Master is an educational Energy Trading & Risk Management simulation and learning platform. We are committed to protecting your privacy. **The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or sensitive user data.**

---

### 2. Information We Do Not Collect
- **Personal Information:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or government identification.
- **Location Information:** The App does not access, collect, or monitor device location data (neither GPS nor network-based location).
- **Financial & Payment Data:** The App is free and does not process financial transactions, bank accounts, or payment card information.
- **Biometric or Health Data:** The App does not collect or access biometric or health data.
- **Device Identifiers:** The App does not collect persistent device identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, advertising ID) for tracking purposes.

---

### 3. On-Device Local Data Storage
The App stores non-personal educational progress data locally on your device using Android's sandboxed local storage (`AsyncStorage`):
- **Quiz Progression:** Category completion status and high scores (e.g. scores required to unlock subsequent modules).
- **Curriculum State:** Bookmarks and completed reading modules.

**All local data remains exclusively on your device.** It is never transmitted to external servers, cloud databases, or third parties. You can clear this data at any time by clearing the app cache/storage in your device settings or using the "Reset Progress" button inside the App.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services and Analytics
- **No Third-Party Ads:** The App contains no third-party advertisements or ad networks.
- **No Tracking SDKs:** The App does not integrate third-party tracking, analytics, or profiling SDKs (such as Facebook SDK or ad attribution trackers).

---

### 5. Permissions
The App requests no sensitive Android permissions (such as Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Location, SMS, or Storage permissions). It functions completely offline.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy
The App does not target or knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements or app features. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
